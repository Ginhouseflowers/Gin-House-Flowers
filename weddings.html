Weddings

Wedding flowers, made for you

Bespoke wedding floristry from our studio in Histon. From the first conversation to the final buttonhole, we design your flowers around your day, your venue and your story.

Start your enquiry Call 01223 656670
  • Free consultation
  • Itemised proposal
  • Delivered on your day

Your wedding flowers should feel unmistakably yours. We take the time to understand your vision, then bring it to life with seasonal flowers, careful detail and a calm, personal service.

Most couples have never ordered flowers on this scale before, so we guide you through every decision: colours, flowers, quantities and budget. We look after everything from your first consultation, through the weeks and days before, to delivery on the day itself.

What we create

Flowers for every part of your day

Choose a few beautiful pieces or dress the whole day. Every design is made to measure for you.

01 · The wedding party

Bouquets & buttonholes

Your bouquet is the piece you will hold all day and see in every photograph. We design it around your dress, your colours and the flowers you love, then carry the look through to your bridesmaids and the rest of the wedding party.

  • Bridal bouquets
  • Bridesmaids’ bouquets
  • Buttonholes
  • Corsages

02 · The ceremony

A beautiful place to say “I do”

Statement flowers that frame your vows and welcome your guests. From moon gate arches to church doorways and college windows, we design to suit the character of your venue.

  • Floral arches & moon gates
  • Doorway & entrance flowers
  • Window & ledge arrangements
  • Ceremony focal pieces

03 · The reception

Tables your guests will remember

Long tables lined with flowers and candlelight, tall centrepieces in a marquee or simple bud vases on every table. We style your reception flowers to complement the room and the mood of your celebration.

  • Table centrepieces
  • Long table arrangements
  • Bud vases
  • Top table flowers

How it works

From first hello to your wedding day

  1. Get in touch

    Send us your date, your venue and a little about your plans, and we will arrange a time to talk.

  2. Free consultation

    We chat through your whole vision: colours, flowers, styles, venue and theme. Bring along any inspiration you love.

  3. Your proposal

    We send a bespoke proposal with images and individual prices for every piece, so you know exactly what you are paying for.

  4. Your wedding day

    We prepare your flowers by hand and deliver them on the day, so you can relax and enjoy every moment.

Questions

Good to know

Anything else on your mind? Call us on 01223 656670 and we will happily help.

How much do wedding flowers cost?

Every wedding is different and flowers change with the seasons, so we price each wedding individually. After your free consultation we send a bespoke proposal with the price of every piece, so you know exactly what you are paying for.

When should we get in touch?

As soon as you have your date and venue. The earlier we hear from you, the more time we have to plan your flowers together.

Do you deliver to our venue?

Yes. We deliver your flowers on your wedding day, and we have created weddings at venues including Haughley Park Barn, South Farm and Sidney Sussex College.

Can you include something personal to us?

Absolutely. We love bringing personal touches into the design, from a favourite flower or colour to flowers that reflect your heritage.
