Flowers change the feel of a space. They welcome your guests, lift your team and show clients the care you put into everything you do.

We talk through what you have in mind, looking at colours, styles and the right arrangements for your space or occasion. From simple bouquets and table flowers to large vase arrangements and statement pieces, we design everything to suit you.

What we offer

Flowers for every part of your business

01 · Displays & entrances

Flowers for your space

Fresh weekly displays for receptions, offices, bars and restaurants, and seasonal arrangements that dress your windows and doors, all designed to showcase the best of each season. We keep a regular rotation of glassware to complement the flowers and your space.

  • Weekly display arrangements
  • Reception & office flowers
  • Seasonal window & door arrangements
  • Flowers & plants for bars and restaurants

02 · Events

Flowers that set the scene

From client dinners to launches and celebrations, we design flowers to suit the occasion and the room, with free glassware hire for any event.

  • Table pieces
  • Centrepieces
  • Focal designs
  • Free glassware hire

03 · Gifts

Team & client gifts

Bespoke bouquets to thank a client, recognise someone in your team, or mark a retirement or a leaver. We can make them in your brand colours, season depending, and deliver to their workplace or their home.

  • Thank you & welcome flowers
  • Retirements & leavers
  • Brand colours, season depending
  • Monthly invoicing for contract customers

04 · Team workshops

A creative afternoon for your team

Our Flower School workshops make a memorable team building or hospitality event. Your team enjoys a drink and a demonstration, then makes an arrangement or wreath to take home, with everything supplied.

Questions

Good to know

Anything else? Call us on 01223 656670.

Do you supply the vases?

Yes. Glassware hire is free for any event, and for regular displays we keep a rotation of glassware to complement the flowers and your space.

Can you match our brand colours?

Yes, season depending. Tell us your colours and we will choose flowers to suit them.

How does invoicing work?

Monthly contract customers receive a monthly invoice on 30 day terms, including any gift bouquets ordered during the month.

Can you deliver gifts to someone’s home?

Yes. We deliver gift bouquets to the workplace or directly to the recipient’s home.

Can you run a workshop for our team?

Absolutely. Our Flower School runs private workshops for team building, corporate entertainment and hospitality events.
