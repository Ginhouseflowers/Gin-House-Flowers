Flower School

Flower arranging workshops

Relaxed, hands-on workshops with our award-winning florists Lucy and Frankie. Bring your enthusiasm and leave with something beautiful you made yourself.

See our classes Book or enquire
  • Two-hour workshops
  • Everything supplied
  • Gift vouchers available

Each workshop is designed with beginners in mind, with plenty to learn whatever your skill level. All you need is a willingness to give it a go.

Choose from an assortment of fabulous flowers and foliage, then create your own design with help on hand and plenty of one-to-one guidance. It is a lovely way to spend an afternoon or evening with friends.

Your workshop

What to expect

  1. A drink on arrival

    Settle in with a glass of bubbles or a G&T.

  2. A demonstration

    We show you how to choose, prepare, wire and arrange your flowers.

  3. Make your own

    Create your design from our flowers and foliage, with one-to-one guidance throughout.

  4. Take it home

    Leave with your finished piece and the know-how to do it again.

All flowers, foliage, materials and equipment are supplied, including the vases, frames or containers you need for your design.

Questions

Good to know

Anything else? Call us on 01223 656670.

Do I need any experience?

Not at all. Our workshops are designed with beginners in mind, and we guide you one-to-one throughout.

What is included?

Everything: flowers, foliage, materials and equipment, including any vases, frames or containers, plus a glass of bubbles or a G&T.

How do I book?

Send us a message, email us or pop into the shop. Christmas wreath places are secured with a £10 deposit.

Can I book a private workshop?

Yes. Private group dates can often be arranged. Tell us about your group and the occasion and we will tailor the workshop for you.

Do you sell gift vouchers?

Yes. Workshop vouchers make a lovely present. Call us or visit the shop to buy one.
