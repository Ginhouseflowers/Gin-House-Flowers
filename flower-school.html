Flower School

Flower arranging workshops

Run by our award-winning florists, Lucy and Frankie, we offer a range of wonderful flowery classes. Bring your enthusiasm and leave with something beautiful you made yourself.

See our classes Book or enquire
  • Beginners welcome
  • Everything supplied
  • Private group classes

Each workshop is designed with novices in mind, but there’s always plenty to learn whatever your skill level. All you need is enthusiasm and a willingness to give it a go!

Our classes make the perfect activity for groups looking for a unique and private celebration, such as a baby shower, Christmas party, hen party or birthday. They are an equally popular choice for corporate entertainment and hospitality showcases too. We can tailor our workshop format to create a bespoke floral experience you’ll never forget.

With an assortment of fabulous flowers and foliage to choose from, you will embark on your own creation with help on hand and plenty of one-to-one guidance.

Christmas wreath workshop

Our Christmas wreath classes have had an upgrade this year! Instead of joining us in the shop, we are joining forces with the Red Lion pub in Histon for a couple of evenings of festive fun. With the bar ready to serve you all the refreshments you could possibly want, we will teach you to create your own fully moss-backed spruce wreath.

To book your spot, call 01223 656670, pop in, or email info@ginhouseflowers.co.uk.

£60 per person · £10 deposit · The Red Lion, Histon

Your workshop

What to expect

  1. Choose your flowers

    Pick from an assortment of fabulous seasonal flowers and foliage.

  2. Learn the techniques

    We show you how to condition, select and arrange your stems.

  3. Make your own

    Create your design from our flowers and foliage, with one-to-one guidance throughout.

  4. Take it home

    Leave with your finished piece and the know-how to do it again.

Good to know

Do I need any experience?

Not at all. Our workshops are designed with beginners in mind, and we guide you one-to-one throughout.

What is included?

All flowers, foliage, materials and equipment are supplied, including the vases, frames or containers you may need for your design.

How do I book?

Call us on 01223 656670, pop into the shop, or email info@ginhouseflowers.co.uk. Christmas wreath places are secured with a £10 deposit per person.

Can I book a private workshop?

Yes. Private group dates can often be arranged. Tell us about your group and the occasion and we will tailor the workshop for you.
