Your flower & gift shop

Nestled in the heart of Histon High Street, just outside Cambridge, our beautiful flower and gift shop is the home for all your floral needs. We have single stem flowers and gift bouquets ready to take away as well as cards and gifts for any occasion. Alongside this, we also specialise in wedding and funeral flowers. Whatever the occasion, our team are on hand to help, support and guide you.
